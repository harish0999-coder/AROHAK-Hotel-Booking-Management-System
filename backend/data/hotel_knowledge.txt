[SECTION: Check-in Policy]
Standard check-in time is 2:00 PM.
Guests must provide a valid government-issued photo ID at check-in.
The name on the booking should match the guest's identification document.
Early check-in is subject to room availability and is not guaranteed.
Early check-in before 10:00 AM may incur a charge of INR 1,500, subject to availability.

[SECTION: Check-out Policy]
Standard check-out time is 12:00 PM.
Late check-out until 2:00 PM is subject to availability and may be complimentary for selected room categories.
Late check-out after 2:00 PM and before 6:00 PM is charged at 50% of the applicable nightly room rate.
Check-out after 6:00 PM is charged at the full applicable nightly room rate.

[SECTION: Guest and Occupancy Policy]
Maximum occupancy depends on the room category.
Children below 6 years can stay in an existing bed at no additional room charge.
An extra bed is available only for selected room categories and must be requested in advance.
The maximum number of occupants must not exceed the room's stated capacity.

[SECTION: Cancellation and Modification Policy]
A customer can directly cancel a booking until 24 hours before the scheduled check-in date.
For a booking with check-in on 20 September, direct cancellation is available until 19 September.
Cancellation requests submitted after the direct-cancellation deadline require staff review.
Approved cancellation requests change the booking status to CANCELLED.
Rejected cancellation requests leave the booking active/confirmed.
A cancelled booking cannot be cancelled again.
Date modifications are subject to room availability and may require staff approval.
The hotel does not guarantee availability after a modification request until the change is confirmed.

[SECTION: Room Categories]
Deluxe King: capacity 2 guests, price INR 8,500 per night. King bed, city view, work desk.
Deluxe Twin: capacity 2 guests, price INR 8,500 per night. Two twin beds, city view, work desk.
Premier Sea View: capacity 3 guests, price INR 12,500 per night. King bed, sea view, sofa chair.
Executive Suite: capacity 3 guests, price INR 18,000 per night. Bedroom, living area, sea view.
Family Suite: capacity 4 guests, price INR 22,000 per night. Two bedrooms, living area, dining table.

[SECTION: Room Amenities]
All room categories include: complimentary Wi-Fi, air conditioning, smart television with local and international channels, electric kettle and tea/coffee setup, in-room safe, hair dryer, mini refrigerator, complimentary bottled water (2 bottles per night), daily housekeeping.
Executive Suite and Family Suite additionally include: separate living area, bathrobe and slippers, premium bathroom amenities, evening turndown service.
